# BIBLE — «الخاتم اللي اتدحرج» (The Ring That Rolled Away)

One sloping city street (kit set `city_street`, bright day) running west→east
to a plaza fountain. A proposal ring escapes at a rehearsal kneel and rolls
the whole street; the chase is the film. All 13 lines are the narrator
(voiceover); nobody speaks on camera. His performed wording (clean.json)
governs; the script of record is script_owner_ar.md, performed text in
script_performed_ar.md, differences in diff_list.md.

## Cast
- **groom** — the young man, navy suit, white shirt; his tie, pocket square
  and the mud on his jacket are PROPS so the suit can be wrecked on camera.
- **bride** — the young woman waiting at the fountain; on screen from the
  fountain beats, face beats only (laugh line 11, yes line 12).
- **The ring** — hero prop. It never vanishes and never teleports: hand →
  ballistic drop → continuous rolling keys → grate hop (drop) → rim flight
  (drop, cartoon gravity) → floats on the fountain water → pick_up → fist.
