## The Slip
He had one knee down and the ring in his hand when the pigeon bumped his elbow.
The ring bounced once, twice, and rolled.
And the whole street ran downhill.

## The Chase
He sprinted after it in his good suit, shouting at nobody.
It hopped the drain grate by a hair.
It threaded two pigeons and a broom.
Every step it rolled faster, and every step he cared less about the suit.
[the ring is tiny — the camera punches in on it at every near miss]

## The Fountain
At the fountain steps it hit the rim and flew.
For one whole second, the ring hung in the air over the water.
He dove.

## The Answer
He came up soaked, ring in his fist, and she was standing there laughing.
She said yes before he even found his knee.
Some rings roll away. His rolled him straight to her.
