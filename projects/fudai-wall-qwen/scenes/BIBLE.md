## Decisions made here first
- Opening reconciliation: frame 0 is the mayor's expressive face, head ≥ 300 px, calm but scared-expressive, with the wall's rise beginning in the same shot (tilt-up from face to wall top, never an empty scene or a back).
- D = real metres × 1.6: wall/gate 15.5 m ≈ 24.8 D; wave 15 m ≈ 24 D; neighbours' walls 10 m = 16 D.
- Places sit 400 D apart, each in its own dome and fog; domes write depth.
- Every line has a place, a beat, an action word and a story prop.
- Captions: `captions: 'words'` — one or two big bold words per beat.
- Sound design is the app's automatic system; no mix or sound files. Clear actions on words and cuts on hits give it something to land on.
