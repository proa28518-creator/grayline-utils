## 1. Format lock
- `fps: 30`, `size: [1080, 1920]` (9:16 vertical) — RULES §10 / film.md.
- Runtime = the voice file's length: **68.08 s**, no trim (voice is law).
- 7 scenes, timings exactly as the bible's scene headers (00:00.10 – 01:07.55).

## 2. Character construction — LOCKED (bible header + characters.md)
- 2.5D flat cartoon people: **big round head, dot eyes (expressive), bean/egg torso,
  thin stick arms and legs, thick black ink outline.**
- Faces **snap between drawings, never tween** (characters.md). Expressions act the spoken words.
- One strong owned colour per main character; no two mains share hat type AND colour.
- Leads: 3–6 torso details. Crowd/extras: 1–3. Never noses, ears, fingers, skin tones,
  gloves, boots; never scale the head (kid build changes limbs, not head).
- Faces read within ~60° of camera, big, never cut by the frame edge (RULES §4).

## 3. The Mayor's aging ladder — LOCKED (resolves bible header vs scenes)
The bible header defines only "short grey hair"; scenes 4 and 6 use other states.
All three are ONE character, one cast spec with three hair states:
- **Young** (S6 flashback): black hair.
- **Prime** (S1–S3, S5-era references): short **grey** hair.
- **Retired** (S4): **white** hair.
Suit, face, stance and owned colour never change across states.

## 4. World zones — LOCKED (bible header)
- **W1 — Coast / village / the Wall:** small fishing village, simple wooden houses, boat
  harbour; the mega-wall: **grey concrete with a steel gate, 15.5 m**, between village and sea.
- **W2 — Flashback + analogy:** old mountain rock with a **carved horizontal wave-mark**;
  warm interior room — a wall, a pencil, a height mark above the child's head.
- **W3 — Neighbours + hill graveyard:** coastal villages with **10 m** walls; a quiet hill
  overlooking the saved village, holding the mayor's simple gravestone.

## 6. Graphic & numeral language — LOCKED
One graphic system across all scenes, Latin digits throughout, as written in the bible:
- S1: transparent 5-floor building diagram beside the wall, floors numbered **1-2-3-4-5**.
- Floating labels: **3,000** (village) · **$30M** + calendar **12** (S2) · **15.5M** glowing
  measure arrow (S2) · **10M** stickers on neighbour walls (S2, S7) · **15M** glowing measure
  line on the wave (S5) · calendar **14** (S5).
- **The wave is 15M; the wall is 15.5M; neighbours are 10M. Never swap, never round.**
- Measure lines/arrows share one glowing style in S2, S5, S7 (the payoff must rhyme with the setup).

## 7. Signature shots (style-bearing, keep as written)
- S1 vertical tilt-up hero reveal + crane pull-back (village reads as a miniature model
  against the wall) + mirror-sea glide.
- S3 signature big close-up, light Dutch angle 5°, mocker laughing full-mouth, pointing
  wall-top → sea; imagined cartoon coins falling into water.
- S3 deadpan medium close-up, slow push-in on the mayor (mouth closed, no argument).
- S3 tight blueprint insert: a hand pulls the height line down; the mayor's hand pins the
  ruler at **15.5M**.
- S6 match-cut dissolve: pencil mark on the room wall → top edge of the concrete wall.
- S7 side-profile impact: wave hits, white spray, camera shake, water rises to the rim —
  and stops. Then crane reveal over the wall: village dry under a warm sunbeam.

## 8. Set-bar implications (RULES §5 — export refuses below it)
Every shot ≥22% detailed set / ≤80% flat; film median ≥28% detail / ≤78% flat; ≤40% of
shots under. Style consequence: wide and aerial shots (S1 pull-back, S5 coastline, S7
aerial) must carry scatter and detail density at scale — the "miniature model" look is
achieved with detail, never with empty flat ground/sea planes.
