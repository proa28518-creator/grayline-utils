- Pixel gate (driver-authored reference) — three defects found by Astra's review (2026-11-23, phase 11): (1) pixel_measure.py CLAMPS expanded disc bounds to the image, making audit_pixels' "disc crosses edge by >24px" check dead code — crop verdicts actually rest on the skin-touch test alone; preserve UNCLIPPED bounds for the cross check. (2) Pair detection passes on one large segmented region — it must match BOTH named characters' discs before passing a two-face moment. (3) Measured-disc overlap has no depth test — an occluder must be CLOSER (larger/nearer disc or z evidence), not merely overlapping, or background discs false-fail occlusion. Any re-derivation must fix all three and still reproduce the validation table.
- Projected bbox "frame share" from a camera sampler does NOT account for occlusion: F590 round 2's sea-plane share read 23.8% in projection but ~7% in the render (the wall and village occlude most of the plane). Treat projected shares of large background planes as upper bounds, never as the frame's measured composition; the pixel gate on the still is the composition judge.
- Astra batch-2 method adoptions (2026-11-23): (1) STANDING: approve staging with projected evidence, then approve the result with rendered pixels — neither substitutes for the other. (3) A head disc out of frame does not prove palm/arm coverage — check the named body part specifically. (4) Occupancy-style measurements use the rendered cluster's aggregate bounds from the sampled eased camera + state at t, never the origin proxy box or the authored TO pose.
