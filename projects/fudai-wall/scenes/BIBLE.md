# BIBLE — «السور اللي وقف الموجة» (Fudai — The Wall That Stopped the Wave)

A fishing village behind a 15.5 m concrete seawall its mayor built over 12 years for
$30M while everyone laughed; he dies unbelieved; 14 years later a 15 m wave stops
exactly at his wall's edge. All 24 lines are the narrator (voiceover, the owner's own
recording); nobody speaks on camera — the cast ACTS every line. The owner's performed
wording governs: script of record `script_owner_ar.md` (186 words verbatim from his
approved word-timestamps file), timings `audio/clean.json` (his words on his own word
times, 0.10–67.55 s), film length = the voice file's 68.08 s exactly. Direction of
record: the owner's 7-scene visualization bible. Style: cartoon-shorts (RULES §11),
2.5D flat characters (big round head, dot eyes, bean body, thin limbs, thick ink
outline; faces SNAP between drawings) on 3D toon sets — STYLE_SHEET.md (Seat 6) locks
the look, the 5 lighting states and the numeral system (Latin digits throughout).

## Scale
Units are D (1 D = one head diameter; a standing adult ≈ 2 D ≈ 1.7 m, so 1 D ≈ 0.86 m).
The wall: **18 D tall** (15.5 m), gate leaf in its centre. Neighbour walls: **12 D**
(10 m). The wave: **17.5 D** (15 m) — it must read BELOW the wall's top edge by the
wall's half-metre margin. Village houses 4–5 D. One scale everywhere (PROP GUARD).

## Cast
- **mayor** — the hero, prime state: short GREY hair, dark municipal suit, upright,
  calm fixed gaze, closed-mouth deadpan. Never argues; his stillness is the acting.
- **mayor_old** — same man, WHITE hair (S4): retirement + grave beats.
- **mayor_young** — same man, BLACK hair (S6 flashback).
  (One character, three cast entries sharing the body spec — Seat 1 ruling 22:15.)
- **mocker_a / mocker_b / mocker_c** — villagers, fisher clothes, warm contrasting
  hats, one owned colour each. Expression arc IS their spec: mockery (S3) → shock
  (S5) → gratitude (S7). **S7's mourners are these same entries** (Seat 1 ruling).
- **villagers** — crowd extras (1–3 torso details each), village square / streets.
- **child** — S6 room beat only: kid build (limbs shortened, head NOT scaled).
- **workers** — construction extras (S2 time-lapse, S6 match-cut pour).
