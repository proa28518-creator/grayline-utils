> The single biggest build risk is synchronizing 34 Arabic narration lines with
> visual pacing across the elevator transitions — misalignment will break the
> emotional arc and make the surreal progression feel jarring rather than
> dreamlike. Stage the build around three anchor keyframes: lobby-to-lobby
> (establishing rhythm), lobby-to-street (reality break), and street-to-stars
> (transcendence), ensuring the narration hits match these transition moments
> before filling intermediate animation. Test the elevator door open/close
> cycles early with temporary audio to lock timing, since that repeated
> mechanical beat is your metronome for everything else.

Desk takeaway: build the three door transitions FIRST against the owner's
timing file; the door cycle is the film's metronome. Everything else fills in
between the anchors.
