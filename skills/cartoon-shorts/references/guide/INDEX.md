# The visual guide pack: 3D Cartoon Shorts (New)

Your quality standard in pictures. Ten pages, one part of production each. Every page: real frames from several films (no -> yes,
the fault named in a few words), rules with the numbers from this skill, and an `<any_topic>` row on how to apply it to a story the
frames do not show. **The examples show the bar, never the subject:** never copy a film, a set or a shot from a page.

**How to use it:** read the pages for the stage you are in before you start it (open the jpg files), and check your frames against
them before you finish it. The bar tiles listed below (in `tiles/`) are the "yes" pictures the critic loop (`references/critic.md`)
shuffles with your frames; every one is from a bar film's finished frames (the button town, s4, backrooms), never a "no".

Per stage, in short: **Build** 01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 09, 10. **Camera angles** 01, 05 (+ 02 when cuts move), 09, 10. **Environment
overhaul** 01, 03, 06, 09, 10. **Add post processing** 01, 07, 09, 10. **Shading pass** 01, 08, 09, 10.

The tiles come from The Town Built Around a Button (the bar, its pass commits re-rendered), the S-4 submarine and the backrooms films
(the bar films of `references/bar/`), and, as "no" tiles only, a kite park film, a roller-coaster pier, a backrooms app test and lab
tests. "Made bad" tiles are the finished button film re-rendered with one setting pushed wrong. No member's video is used. Only the
bar tiles ship here; the full sources (html, every tile) are in the Creatorize repository's `docs/guide-pack/cartoon/`.
